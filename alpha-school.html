A scalable rate card for Alpha School’s larger schools

Nathan Bingle Photography is a Charlotte-based, high-volume school and event photography company serving the greater Carolinas. This proposal is a simple school-paid rate card for Alpha School’s larger campuses and network events — staff or campus headshots priced per person, plus on-site event coverage billed by the hour. It is not a parent-pay picture-day program and is not locked to a single campus headcount.

We’re a Member of the Professional Photographers of America, with 5+ years specializing in K–12 and campus photography across the region. COI, background checks, and clear invoices per campus or event are available on request.

Per-person headshots

$45 per person. Consistent, polished portraits for websites, directories, and PR — digital delivery included. Scale to whatever roster each campus needs.

Hourly event coverage

$400 first hour, then $250 each additional hour. Candids and program moments for Alpha’s channels — book the block that fits each event.

Network-ready billing

Same rates across larger schools. Invoice per campus or per event from the rate card — no parent envelopes, no fundraising dial.

FAQ

Answered before they’re asked

Is this a parent-pay picture day?
No. This is a school-paid rate card for headshots and event coverage across Alpha School’s larger campuses. Alpha is invoiced from the rates above — families are not asked to order packages.
Is pricing locked to one campus or a fixed total?
No. Headshots are always $45 per person and coverage is always $400 for the first hour plus $250 for each additional hour. Totals scale with each school’s roster and the hours you book. Any dollar example in this proposal is illustrative only.
Can different Alpha schools use the same rates?
Yes. This rate card is intended for Alpha School’s larger schools and network events. Confirm headcount and hours per campus; we invoice from the same published rates.
Background checks and insurance?
Every on-site team member maintains current background checks. We carry $2,000,000 commercial general liability and provide a Certificate of Insurance to Alpha School on request.
How do we book the next campus or event?
Share the campus, preferred date, approximate headshot count, and coverage hours with Gaby’s team or directly with Nathan. We confirm availability and send a matching invoice from this rate card.
