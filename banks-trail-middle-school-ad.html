FAQ

Likely questions, answered.

What's the AD's actual workload during a typical season?
Provide a roster, point us at a space on picture day, and approve a parent communication. We do the rest — including handling parent service issues directly so they don't reach your office. Most AD partners spend under 90 minutes per season actively interacting with the photo program.
What if my coaches have specific style preferences?
We accommodate. Some sports want clean classic team photos, others want dramatic composites, others want the natural-light outdoor look. Coaches tell us what they want, we deliver. No drama.
Is Banks Trail MS on the hook for any cost?
No. There's no fee, retainer, or minimum spend. The school is never invoiced. Parents pay only for portraits they choose to order, directly through the online gallery.
How is student data protected?
Student data Banks Trail MS provides is used solely to fulfill the photography program. We do not sell, lease, or share it. All school-provided data is deleted or returned within 30 days of agreement termination, except as needed to fulfill open parent orders or as required by law.
