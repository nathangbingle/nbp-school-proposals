About This Brief

A photography program built around the Hive

This page is for CVA leadership and board members reviewing the proposed photography partnership with Nathan Bingle Photography for the 2026–27 school year. It summarizes what CVA gets, what the school earns, and what the formal agreement covers.

Nathan Bingle Photography is a Charlotte-based, high-volume school and youth sports photography company serving the greater Carolinas. We're a Member of the Professional Photographers of America and operate a streamlined picture-day workflow specifically designed for schools — including non-traditional environments like virtual academies.

CVA's blended model means picture day works differently than a traditional school. We've structured this partnership around how the Hive actually operates: in-person Thursdays, voluntary participation, and a parent-facing experience that doesn't put any work on Mrs. Rotan or her staff.

How Picture Day Works

Light lift for CVA. Heavy lift for us.

A picture day at a virtual school looks different — and that's the point. Here's the four-step rhythm:

1
Schedule
CVA shares student data and the in-person Thursday date 2–3 weeks ahead. NBP builds an appointment-based schedule.
2
Notify
NBP supplies pre-written email and text templates. CVA sends through ParentSquare, "The Hive," and existing channels.
3
Shoot
NBP arrives with photographers and full setup. Sessions run on the Weddington Road campus during the in-person day.
4
Deliver
Galleries go out within ~2 weeks. Parents order, NBP fulfills and ships. CVA receives commission report and school-use images.
What CVA Staff Actually Has To Do

Provide student/parent contact data, share the appointment schedule with families, give NBP access to space on picture day. That's it. No order collection, no money handling, no parent service issues, no fulfillment.

Commission Structure

Tiered commission, paid annually

CVA earns commission on aggregate net portrait sales across all sessions in the school year (fall, winter, and spring combined). The more participation, the higher the tier — giving CVA an active stake in promotion.

Net sales = gross sales minus taxes, shipping, processing fees, refunds, and production costs. Commissions paid within 60 days of the final session archive, with a full itemized sales report.

A Note on Senior Sitting Fees

The $25 senior booking deposit is an operational fee — it covers session time and scheduling overhead, similar to how a doctor's office handles appointment holds. It's not a portrait sale, so it isn't part of commission. All senior portrait orders placed through the online gallery are commissioned at the same rates above.

Likely Board Questions

Answered before they're asked

Is CVA on the hook for any cost?
No. There is no fee, retainer, or minimum spend. The school is never invoiced. Parents pay only for portraits they choose to order, directly through NBP's gallery.
What happens if participation is low?
Commission scales with sales — there's no penalty to CVA if a season is light. Both parties agree in good faith to reschedule sessions disrupted by weather, illness, or low turnout under the force majeure section.
How is student data protected?
Student data CVA provides (names, parent contact) is used solely to fulfill the photography program. NBP does not sell, lease, or share it for unrelated marketing. All school-provided data is deleted or returned within 30 days of agreement termination, except as needed to fulfill open parent orders or as required by law.
Can NBP use student photos in its marketing?
Industry-standard release: NBP may use images for its own marketing and portfolio only when a signed parent release is on file for the depicted student. Both CVA and individual parents may flag opt-outs at any time, and those are honored.
What if CVA wants to exit the agreement?
Either party can terminate for convenience with 30 days' written notice. Material breach can be terminated immediately if not cured within 15 days. Commission earned on completed sales prior to termination is paid out per the standard payment schedule.
How does NBP handle the "virtual" reality of CVA?
Sessions are scheduled on a designated in-person Thursday on the Weddington Road campus, with appointment-based slots so families can plan around their day. The January make-up date catches new enrollments and absences — a structure built specifically for the way CVA operates.
