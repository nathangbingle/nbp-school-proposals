What's included at every partner school
School Picture Day
Individual portraits, classroom group composites, staff portraits, and retake day — run on a tight schedule that keeps disruption to the school day minimal.
Athletics, Every Season
Team and individual photos for every sport the school runs — fall, winter, and spring. Same consistent quality, same streamlined workflow every time.
Free Fall Banner Program
A free 24×36 custom banner for every 8th-grade fall athlete. Winter and spring banners at $10 each — approximately half of typical market rate.
Revenue Share Back to the School
A percentage of every photo sale returns to the school or booster program as a fundraising commission. Transparent, itemized reporting with every check.
Online Ordering, Shipped to Families
Parents order online at their own pace. Photos ship directly to their door. No order forms, no cash collection, no envelopes for the front office to manage.
One Partner, Every Program
Consolidate picture day, sports, and banner needs with a single vendor across every school in the district. One contact, one workflow, one standard.

We can offer this because our operation is built for volume. At scale, the math works — and the school gets the benefit.

Most school photographers can't offer this. A free fall-banner program and half-market winter/spring pricing only works at a photography operation engineered for it: streamlined picture days, optimized ordering systems, and the volume to print banners at a scale that smaller studios can't match.

We're a Fort Mill-based business serving schools and sports programs across the Carolinas — already the photographer at Pleasant Knoll Middle School and at youth sports organizations throughout York, Lancaster, and Mecklenburg counties.

Bringing this program to Fort Mill Schools gives the district a consistent photography standard, a meaningful fundraising return, and a signature fall banner tradition that parents, students, and staff will love — with zero budget impact.
