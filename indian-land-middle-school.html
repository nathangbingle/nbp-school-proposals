How Picture Day Works

Light lift for Indian Land Middle. Heavy lift for us.

Picture day should run on its own. Here’s the four-step rhythm we use for every school partner:

1
Schedule
Indian Land Middle shares the class roster and picture day date. We build the day’s schedule and coordinate with your front office.
2
Notify
We supply pre-written email, text, and flyer templates. Indian Land Middle sends through your preferred parent channels.
3
Shoot
We arrive with photographers and full setup. Every class rotates through quickly — typically done before lunch.
4
Deliver
Galleries go out within ~2 weeks. Parents order, we fulfill and ship. Indian Land Middle receives commission report and school-use images.
What Indian Land Middle staff actually has to do

Provide student/parent contact data, share the picture day notice with families, give us access to space on picture day. That’s it. No order collection, no money handling, no parent service issues, no fulfillment.

Commission Flex

Indian Land Middle controls the revenue-share rate

The prices above are our standard retail, already structured with a 20% commission share to Indian Land Middle built in. But Indian Land Middle can adjust that rate up or down — season to season — to match what the school wants at any given time.

How It Works
Dial left for the lowest family prices.
Dial right for the biggest fundraiser.

Every dollar a family spends (less sales tax and shipping) is split between Indian Land Middle and NBP according to the commission rate Indian Land Middle selects. Full itemized reporting every time — every student, every order, every dollar.

10%Lowest prices
20%Standard
30%Moderate lift
40%Strong fundraiser
50%Maximum
Example: At Indian Land Middle’s standard 20% rate, the Mid Tier package is $45 and Indian Land Middle earns $9 per order. Dial to 30%, and the Mid Tier becomes ~$52 with $16 going to the school. Dial to 40%, and it’s ~$60 with $24 to the school. Every tier available at any package.
Transparent. Honest. No Hidden Exclusions.

We pay commission on every package and every a-la-carte item. No products quietly excluded. No fine print about “qualifying orders.” If a family spends a dollar, Indian Land Middle earns on every one — less only state sales tax and shipping.

Likely Questions

Answered before they’re asked

If this proposal gets shared with admin, board, or district, these are the questions that usually come up first.

Is Indian Land Middle on the hook for any cost?
No. There is no fee, retainer, or minimum spend. The school is never invoiced. Parents pay only for portraits they choose to order, directly through our online gallery.
What happens if participation is low?
Commission scales with sales — there’s no penalty to Indian Land Middle if a season is light. We’ve worked with schools of every size and know how to drive participation through parent communication support.
How is student data protected?
Student data Indian Land Middle provides (names, parent contact) is used solely to fulfill the photography program. We do not sell, lease, or share it for unrelated marketing. All school-provided data is deleted or returned within 30 days of agreement termination, except as needed to fulfill open parent orders or as required by law.
Can NBP use student photos in its marketing?
Industry-standard release: we may use images for our own marketing and portfolio only when a signed parent release is on file for the depicted student. Both Indian Land Middle and individual parents may flag opt-outs at any time, and those are honored.
Background checks and safeguarding?
Every NBP photographer and on-site staff member maintains current background checks and adheres to applicable child safeguarding standards, including reasonable supervision practices during on-site sessions.
How does this fit a growing school?
We scale with you. Whether Indian Land Middle has 200 students now or 600 at full enrollment, the same process runs at each picture day. Commission tiers and service menu grow with the school.
