The Program

A photo program built for CHAMP Sports Fall

Nathan Bingle Photography is a Fort Mill–based, high-volume youth sports photography company serving the greater Charlotte area and the Carolinas. We’re a Member of the Professional Photographers of America, with 5+ years specializing in high-volume team and individual photography across the region.

Prepared for CHAMP Sports (Calvary Church). This is a parent-pay picture day proposal for Fall youth athletics — individuals and teams — for CHAMP Sports programs at Calvary Church. It is not school-district vendor language and does not claim school-system affiliation. We schedule around your Fall season calendar — with Saturday, October 10 at Calvary Life Center as the first locked picture day — so every team gets covered without slowing down games or practices.

Zero League Cost

No fees, no upfront spend, no minimums. Parents pay only if they choose to buy. CHAMP Sports never invoices a family or handles a transaction.

Fast Photo Days

Whole teams move through in minutes. Multiple setups, organized lines, and a crew that has done this at volume for years.

Background-Checked · $2M Insured

How It Works

Light lift for CHAMP Sports. Heavy lift for us.

Photo day should run on its own — and never slow down a game schedule. Here’s the four-step rhythm we use for every league partner:

1
Schedule
CHAMP Sports shares rosters and the Fall season calendar. We build a parent-pay picture day plan around sites, practices, and game days (including Saturday, October 10 at Calvary Life Center), then coordinate with league admin.
2
Notify
We supply pre-written email, text, and flyer templates. Coaches and team parents get everything they need — no chasing.
3
We arrive with photographers and full setups. Teams rotate through in minutes — games and practices stay on schedule.
4
Deliver
Galleries go out within ~2 weeks. Parents order, we fulfill and ship straight to their door — nobody at CHAMP Sports touches an order.
What CHAMP Sports actually has to do

Provide rosters and parent contact data, share the photo day notice through your coaches, and point teams at the right spot on the day. That’s it. No order collection, no money handling, no parent service issues, no fulfillment.

Pricing · Parent-Pay

No cost to CHAMP Sports. Families order online.

Packages start at $25 with a-la-carte options for every budget. Families order what they want online — no envelopes, no cash collection. CHAMP Sports also earns a share of every order; we keep the rate simple, put it in writing, and send an itemized report each season.
