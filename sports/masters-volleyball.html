How media day works

Light lift for MVA. Heavy lift for us.

It runs a lot like last December, just with a bigger setup and more time for every player.

01

Pick a date

Once uniforms are in, send a couple of evenings when the gym is free. Weekday evenings are easy for us.

02

Send rosters

Rosters with parent emails and jersey numbers about two weeks out. We build the team-by-team schedule.

03

Media day

We arrive about 45 minutes early to set lights and fog. Teams rotate through on schedule, in game uniforms.

04

Galleries

Edited private galleries go out to families in about two weeks.

What MVA does: pick the date, send rosters and point teams to the court. What we do: everything else, including setup, posing, editing, galleries and parent questions.

Good questions

Answered up front

Can you really match MVA's colors?

Yes. We gel the lights and color the smoke to the club. The Legion photos above show their blue and green. Yours would be MVA purple and green.

What kind of gym works best?

Any gym where we can turn down the house lights. Our lights and fog do the rest. A court like last year's at Rock Hill Sports & Event Center works well.

What should players wear?

Game jerseys, spandex and court shoes. Hair and jersey ready to go, and a backup jersey is always smart.

How long until families see photos?

About two weeks. Every player gets her own private gallery link, sent straight to her family.
