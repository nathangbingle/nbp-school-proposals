How Photo Day Works

Light lift for St. Matthew Catholic Church. Heavy lift for us.

Photo day should run on its own — and never slow down a game schedule. Here’s the four-step rhythm we use for every league partner:

1
Schedule
St. Matthew Catholic Church shares rosters and the season calendar. We build a photo day plan per site and coordinate with your board.
2
Notify
We supply pre-written email, text, and flyer templates. Coaches and team parents get everything they need — no chasing.
3
Shoot
We arrive with photographers and full setups. Teams rotate through in minutes — games and practices stay on schedule.
4
Deliver
Galleries go out within ~2 weeks. Parents order, we fulfill and ship straight to their door — nobody at St. Matthew Catholic Church touches an order.

Likely Questions

Answered before they’re asked

If this proposal gets shared with your board or league leadership, these are the questions that usually come up first.

Is St. Matthew Catholic Church on the hook for any cost?
No. There is no fee, retainer, or minimum spend. The league is never invoiced. Parents pay only for photos they choose to order, directly through our online gallery.
What happens if it rains on photo day?
A makeup date is built into every outdoor schedule at no charge. If a session washes out, we coordinate the new date with your board and re-notify families — nobody scrambles and nobody gets skipped.
How do you handle multiple parks or gyms?
We staff parallel setups and build the schedule around your sites and game calendar. Most leagues get every team photographed in a single organized weekend, with a makeup window behind it.
What’s the workload for our volunteers and coaches?
Share rosters, approve a parent notice, and point teams at the right spot on photo day. We handle scheduling, parent communication templates, ordering, fulfillment, and every parent service issue directly.
How is participant data protected?
Roster data St. Matthew Catholic Church provides (names, parent contact) is used solely to fulfill the photography program. We do not sell, lease, or share it for unrelated marketing. All league-provided data is deleted or returned within 30 days of agreement termination, except as needed to fulfill open parent orders or as required by law.
Can NBP use player photos in its marketing?
Industry-standard release: we may use images for our own marketing and portfolio only when a signed parent release is on file for the depicted player. Both St. Matthew Catholic Church and individual parents may flag opt-outs at any time, and those are honored.
