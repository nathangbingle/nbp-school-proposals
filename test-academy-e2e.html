About This Proposal

A photography program built for Test

Nathan Bingle Photography is a Charlotte-based, high-volume school and youth sports photography company serving the greater Carolinas. We deliver a streamlined, professional picture-day experience designed to minimize disruption for Test and maximize ordering convenience for families.

Why This Works

Designed for a school like yours

Zero School Cost

No fees, no upfront spend, no minimums. Parents pay only if they choose to buy. Test never invoices a family or handles a transaction.

Flexible Commission

Revenue-share rate adjustable from 10–50%, per session. Dial up for fundraising, dial down for family-friendly pricing.

Zero Hassle

We handle parent notices, online ordering, delivery, direct-to-home shipping, and service. Your staff never lifts a finger on fulfillment.

Background-Checked

Every team member maintains current background checks before they ever set foot on Test's campus.

$2M Insured

$2,000,000 in commercial general liability coverage. Certificate of Insurance provided on request.

FERPA-Aligned

Student data used only to fulfill the program. Deleted within 30 days of agreement end. No third-party sharing.

Commission Flex

Test controls the revenue-share rate

The prices above are our standard retail at a 20% commission share to Test. But Test can adjust that rate up or down — session to session.

How It Works
Dial left for the lowest family prices.
Dial right for the biggest fundraiser.

Every dollar a family spends (less sales tax and shipping) is split between Test and NBP according to the commission rate Test selects. Full itemized reporting every time.

10%Lowest
20%Standard
30%Moderate
40%Strong
50%Maximum
Example: At the standard 20% rate, the Mid Tier package is $45 and Test earns $9 per order. Dial to 30%, and it becomes ~$52 with $16 going to the school. Dial to 40%, and it's ~$60 with $24 to the school.
